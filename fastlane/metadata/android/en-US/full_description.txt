A voice recorder that gets out of the way. Every recording lives in one list, a tap unfolds the player right inside the row, and the red button at the bottom starts a new one.

Voice Memos has no ads, no trackers, no analytics and no network access whatsoever. It does not even ask for the internet permission. Your recordings stay on your device, in your own audio folder.

<b>Recording</b>
* A live waveform and a timer down to hundredths of a second
* Keeps recording in the background when you leave the app
* Pause and resume, or discard a take you do not want
* AAC (M4A), AMR-NB, AMR-WB and Opus, in three quality levels
* Stereo recording, skip silence and Bluetooth microphone support
* Optionally pauses on an incoming call

<b>Playback</b>
* Scrub along the waveform
* Skip back and forward 15 seconds
* Playback speed of 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x or 2x
* Media notification controls

<b>Trim editor</b>
Drag the yellow handles over the waveform to pick a section, then trim to keep it or delete to remove it. Edits are previewed before anything is written, and the result is always saved as a new recording, so the original is never touched.

<b>Organise</b>
* Search, rename, favourite and share recordings
* Swipe a row for quick actions
* Recently Deleted keeps removed recordings for 30 days so they can be recovered
* Home screen widgets for your latest recordings and a one tap record button
* Light, dark or automatic appearance

<b>Permissions</b>
* Microphone, to record
* Audio files, to list and play your recordings
* Notifications, for the recording and playback controls
* Phone state, optional, to pause during a call
* Approximate location, optional, to tag M4A recordings with where they were made. It uses the location built into Android, not Google Play Services
